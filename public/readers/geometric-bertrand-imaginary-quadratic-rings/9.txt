4  Further Directions


The thickness hypothesis ensures that the region contains an annular sector of uniform width. It would be

useful to determine whether this hypothesis can be weakened.

   Another problem is to obtain explicit bounds for N0(K, τ, η, c), possibly under the generalized Riemann

hypothesis.
