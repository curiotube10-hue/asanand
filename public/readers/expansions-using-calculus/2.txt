                            ∞ (−1)nx2n

However, do you know where they come from?


2  Polynomial Approximations

Let’s try to approximate ex as a polynomial. Say


                             ∞
                                            ex = X anxn
                                             n=0

Plugging in x = 0,

                                               a0 = 1


Now, we differentiate both sides to obtain


                            ∞
                                          ex = X nanxn−1
                                            n=0

Again, plugging in x = 0,

                                               a1 = 1


Repeating this process of differentiating and plugging in 0, we will get


                                                1
                                          an =
                                                      n!


Try to prove this using induction.
