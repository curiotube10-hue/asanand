2  Practise Problems


Here are some practise problems, in increasing order of difficulty.

   1. IMO 2007 P4

     In triangle ABC the bisector of angle BCA intersects the circumcircle again at R, the perpendicular
      bisector of BC at P, and the perpendicular bisector of AC at Q. The midpoint of BC is K and the
     midpoint of AC is L. Prove that the triangles RPK and RQL have the same area.

   2. IMO 2010 P4

     Let P be a point inside the triangle ABC. The lines AP, BP and CP intersect the circumcircle Γ of
      triangle ABC again at the points K, L and M respectively. The tangent to Γ at C intersects the line
   AB at S. Suppose that SC = SP. Prove that MK = ML.

   3. IMO 2015 P3

     Let ABC be an acute triangle with AB > AC. Let Γ be its circumcircle, H its orthocentre, and F
     the foot of the altitude from A. Let M be the midpoint of BC. Let Q be the point on Γ such that
   ∠HQA = 90◦, and let K be the point on Γ such that ∠HKQ = 90◦. Assume that the points A, B, C, K
     and Q are all different, and lie on Γ in this order. Prove that the circumcircles of triangles KQH and
   FKM are tangent to each other.
