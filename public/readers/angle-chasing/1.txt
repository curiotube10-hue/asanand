  IMO 2018 P1

  Example 1: Let Γ be the circumcircle of acute triangle ABC. Points D and E are on segments AB
   and AC respectively such that AD = AE. The perpendicular bisectors of BD and CE intersect minor
   arcs AB and AC of Γ at points F and G respectively. Prove that lines DE and FG are either parallel
   or they are the same line.


Proof: Extend FD, GE to X, Y on Γ.
∠AXF = ∠ABF = ∠FDB = ∠ADX so AD = AX = AY .

So, Y, D, E, X are concyclic with circumcenter A.
Now, ∠Y ED = ∠Y XD = ∠Y XF = ∠Y GF.
Corresponding angles are equal, so DE ∥FG□.

   Indian TST 2019 Practice Test 2 P1

  Example 2: Let the points O and H be the circumcenter and orthocenter of an acute angled triangle
  ABC. Let D be the midpoint of BC. Let E be the point on the angle bisector of ∠BAC such that
  AE ⊥HE. Let F be the point such that AEHF is a rectangle. Prove that D, E, F are collinear.


Proof: Let XY Z be the orthic triangle, AF ∩Y Z = U, and EF ∩Y Z = V .

Notice that A, H, E, F, Y, Z are concyclic with centre U, and B, C, Y, Z are concyclic with centre D. Addi-
tionally,
             ∠ZV E = 180◦−∠Y ZE −∠FEZ = 180◦−∠ZAE −∠FAZ = 90◦
Thus, D, E, F, and G all lie on the perpendicular bisector of the radical axis Y Z, so we’re done. □
