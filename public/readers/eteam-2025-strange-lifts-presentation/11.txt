   By the Senge-Strauss Theorem, the number of integers the sum of
    whose digits in each of the bases p and q lies below a fixed bound
      is finite if and only if p and q are multiplicatively independent.
    So, dAq(pn) + dAp(pn) = dAq(pn) + 1 ≤2C + 1 for finitely many n.
    However, there are infinite choices for n, contradiction.
